Bored of Dinner

Account deletion

You can delete your Bored of Dinner account from the app by opening Account and choosing Delete Account, then completing the confirmation steps.

Can't access the app?

You can request deletion by emailing us from the email address associated with your Bored of Dinner account. Please include “Account Deletion Request” in the subject line and tell us the email address used for your account. Do not send us your password. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email an account deletion request

Email: Contactus@boredofdinner.co.uk

What data is deleted?

When an account deletion is completed through the app, the Bored of Dinner sign-in account, its profile record, and its associated subscription record in our app database are deleted.

Shared household data: household board content is stored separately and may be shared with other household members. Deleting an individual account does not automatically delete that shared board or its content. If you also want household board data considered for deletion, state this in your email request; we may need to check whether other household members rely on it.

Timing and subscriptions

We aim to process verified email deletion requests within 30 days. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and record-keeping purposes; if this applies, we will explain what is retained and why.

Deleting your Bored of Dinner account does not itself cancel a subscription managed by Google Play, Apple, or another billing provider. Please cancel that subscription directly with the provider as well, if applicable.

For account deletion requests and questions, contact Contactus@boredofdinner.co.uk.
